## Direction contract

THESIS: The home page is one engineering drawing on a cutting mat. A single copper pen line starts as the real PID control law from Nafis's line-follower and becomes the control loop, then the track, then the robot following it: "equations on paper to machines". It refuses the dark-lab hero with neon and the portrait-plus-headline split.

OWN-WORLD: Self-healing cutting-mat green (deep #1E4A3B family) with a chalk-white centimetre grid, ruler ticks on the edges and 45° guides. Chalk-white ink, PCB-copper accent, pencil-grey annotations. Project pages are white datasheets laid on the mat, with title blocks, BOM tables and status stamps. Type: Barlow Semi Condensed (DIN drawing lettering) for display, Source Sans 3 for body, Kalam for hand annotations, JetBrains Mono only for values (dates, part numbers).

STORY: In the first seconds, the visitor sees who Nafis is, what he builds, and how theory becomes hardware. They pick one project's datasheet to read its process, then email or check GitHub.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed mat with the grid. A thin title-block navigation strip runs across the top. On the left, about 55% width: the name at display scale, a one-line engineering statement, an interests line, then the "Explore projects" button (copper) and "View CV". On the right: the drawing. The PID equation is handwritten and resolves into a block diagram, then a track, then the robot, drawn in one stroke with Kalam annotations.

FORM: Grounded direction #1 of 7 (the engineer's cutting mat and drawing sheet). concept-seed was not run: the owner's brief (website-arcitecture.md) pins the visual direction, and new-work rules that a brief-pinned direction beats the roll. seed: none (roll skipped, direction pinned by the brief)
